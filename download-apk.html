Installation Guide for APK Files

Android phones often prevent installing apps from sources other than the Google Play Store for security. This is normal. Follow these simple steps to enable "Unknown Sources" and get your app installed.

  1. 1. Click 'Download APK'

    Click the big blue button above to download the APK file to your device. Look for the 'Downloading' notification.

  2. 2. Go to 'Settings'

    On your Android phone, find and open the main 'Settings' application.

  3. 3. Select 'Security' or 'Apps'

    Search for and select 'Security' (or 'Apps & Notifications' on some devices).

  4. 4. Enable 'Install Unknown Apps'

    Look for 'Special app access' or 'Install unknown apps'. Choose your browser (like Chrome) and toggle 'Allow from this source'.

  5. 5. Locate and Install the APK

    Go to your phone's 'Downloads' folder or file manager. Find the downloaded APK file and click it to start the final installation.

  6. 6. App is Ready!

    Once installation finishes, you can find the app icon and start coding!

Important Tip: For better security, after installation, you can go back and disable the 'Install unknown apps' permission for your browser.
